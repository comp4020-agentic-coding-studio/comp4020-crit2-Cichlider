Why this is different from the real MyTimetable

Swapping happens on the grid, not on a separate screen
The real MyTimetable's swap flow takes you off the calendar into a list of activity codes and times you have to mentally re-plot. Here, clicking an option moves the block on the grid you're already looking at.
A clash is a banner, not a support ticket
Try picking the COMP4020 Wednesday tutorial and the COMP3120 Wednesday lab together — both sit at 14:00, and the grid flags it immediately instead of leaving you to notice the overlap yourself.
